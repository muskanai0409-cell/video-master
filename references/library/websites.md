# Websites: every design, motion and creative site she bookmarked or sent

Every website from her 604 X bookmarks (full post text with the real links, all 604 checked on 2026-10-06) and from every link she sent in her own messages, deduplicated by site. AI news, hackathons, unrelated product launches and article sources were left out: 328 sites were found and the ones below are the design, motion, video and visual ones. The short source labels link to the post each site came from.

**How to use it.** Take the mechanism (a transition, a pacing pattern, a layout idea, a prompt structure), never the palette, fonts, logos, layout or footage. Ask before downloading anything or installing a tool. Self-promotion and referral links are labelled.
