> **Method note.** Frames come from exact-seek 16-frame sheets (tile k at (k+0.5)·T/16 with T capped at 240s, so for the seven files longer than 240s the sheet covers only the first 240s), hook strips (0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5s) and end strips (the last 4s).
