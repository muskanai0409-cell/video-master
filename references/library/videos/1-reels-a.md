**Lessons:**
1. **State the premise within 2 seconds, in words or on a surface viewers already know.** Weave has "$13.5M" up by 0.5s, Lenny rebuts the objection with a sticker at 2s, starks_arq's question is complete at 1s, and Sidehoe's inbox reads at frame 0. Slow opens (RRSI, the café clip) only work when the post copy does the work.
2. **Give the eye one constant.** In films without cuts, one object persists or travels: waveform → peaks → a check mark (tibo_maker), skater → vortex (starks_arq), the URL (gauravsbuilding), the dots (abdinmotion), the brick pyramid (Context Cloud). In a fast montage, keep one shape in one place: the Claude clip cuts every 0.4s around a fixed arc under one 4-word line.
3. **Pair every claim with exactly one proof shot, and show the output in use.** Zaro alternates headline cards and UI, then lands the answer in a chat app. mattyp gives each feature one UI card. Weave shows logos and "ship 19% faster", then the real dashboard. shotbase ends its flow with a clip answering a chat question.
4. **Show agent work as a growing list of status lines or messages from named agents.** Examples are Runable's chip stack (module label plus meta line), Zaro's "Reading sources… / Searching 2142 Files…" and Shipper's timestamped overnight messages. This is the most direct template for showing memory operations (recall, update, forget).
5. **Make colour carry structure, and spend one accent on the idea.** supermemory changes its grade at the reveal. mattyp puts every UI card on a bright gradient. RRSI keeps its teal/yellow/red meanings for 8 minutes and reuses them on its last card. tibo_maker and starks_arq use their only accent on the key mechanism.
6. **Make quantities countable and end on a contrast.** Lenny pairs an "n / 11" counter with a "$40,000+" total, starks_arq ends on "90 years. 4 days.", and leomeethewoo, Shipper and gauravsbuilding roll their numbers like odometers.
7. **Past ~60s, give viewers a map and captions.** The PG essay uses a chapter chip, a progress bar and caption pills. abdinmotion labels a timeline. Context Cloud puts chapter titles behind its speaker. supermemory, Weave and both Dhravya films burn in subtitles for muted autoplay.
8. **End on one clear job, and hold it.** The CTA endings make the next step concrete: Lenny's URL button plus "LIMITED SUPPLIES", Zaro's "Try for free" label, Shipper's prompt box clicked by the cursor, Weave's self-rewriting demo line. Hold the finished card 2s or more (gauravsbuilding gives its lockup ~0.5s). For autoplay loops, make the last frame match frame 0 (Elera, Claude).
