# Recipe: launch film

A designed film that announces one new thing: a product, a feature, an integration or a raise. It runs 20 to 120s and is the most common format in her bookmarks (59 of 304).

**Use it when** there is something new with a name and one task that proves it.

**Use something else when the point is:**

| The point | Recipe |
|---|---|
| how something works | `explainer.md` |
| a list of small changes | `changelog-update.md` |
| several features or agents in a row | `ui-morph-reel.md` |
| a person's story | `talking-head.md` |

**On supermemory** SKILL.md sections 1 and 2 still apply:
- problem first;
- 16:9;
- the site surface or the brand film;
- rebuilt vector UI;
- one accent for the proof;
- a still end hold.

The "viral" register has its own sub-skill in her pack, `quirky-product-reels`.

## Targets (59 launch films, medians)

| | All | 500K+ views (20) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 58s | 76s | Designed launches run 29 to 56s and founder-led ones 65 to 200s. Her 16:9 cap is about 25 to 30s, unless comprehension needs more |
| Moving | 76% | 66% | 70 to 80% |
| Longest still | 1.4s | 1.4s | about 1s before the end card |
| Cuts per minute | 9.6 | 12.8 | UI-led launches 0 to 4; footage, 3D and AI-footage launches 13 to 26 |
| Median shot | 4.0s | 3.3s | |
| Loudness | -14.6 LUFS | -14.9 | Music-led -10 to -14; founder dialogue -16 to -23 |

Craft does not predict views: see `video-study.md` section 2. Announcements from big accounts, with people on screen and borrowed recognition, drew the most views.

## Pick the shape

| Shape | Length | When |
|---|---|---|
| A. Designed launch | 29 to 56s | The default for a product, feature or integration |
| B. Founder-led | 65 to 200s | A company launch or a raise, with a founder who has a story |
| C. Cinematic bookends | 50 to 120s | A premium brand moment, where the opening motif returns at the end |
| D. Manifesto or trailer | 40 to 80s | A category claim. The fast-cut ones in the set drew 22K to 131K views, so use with care |

## Beat template: shape A, about 30s

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 1.5s | Hook | The problem or the news in words, a number changing, or the brand mark already turning into the product. Never a static logo or a fade from black |
| 1.5 to 3s | Proof object | Partner logos, stars, a price, or a source receipt |
| 3 to 8s | The old way | The pain drawn as overload: a cascade of windows from several vendors (5#4), a dashboard window the cursor closes (6#6), a counter of waste |
| 8 to 10s | The turn | "Introducing" and the name, typed or built out of the overload, with one accent word |
| 10 to 22s | One task, end to end | A prompt at readable or giant size, then the work made visible (a status stack, a lane or a counter), then a structured result with one number in the accent. 6#5 does question, "$1,240", Allow click, "Done!" in about 6s |
| 22 to 26s | Breadth | A real list scrolled, a logo ring, or 3 to 4 extras at about 1s each, then "…and N more" |
| 26 to 30s | CTA and lockup | The URL or install command on its own card for 2 to 3s, then the logo held 3 to 5s |

**Shape B (founder-led):**
- Name the product at 10 to 15% of the runtime (5#1 does it at 14s of 94).
- Run UI inserts of 3 to 6s with burned-in captions.
- Put a proof block (stats, logos, testimonials) at 60 to 80% of the runtime.
- Close on the founder, then the lockup with an availability line.

## Hooks that fit

- A typed claim on a plain ground, complete by 1.5s (5#21: one word every 0.5s).
- A sentence with something live inside it: integration swaps "Muse + Google Ads / + Meta Ads" (6#5); a magnifier swapping AI logos (6#6).
- A headline number counting, huge and cropped, then pulling back to say what it is (2#11, 5#25).
- A news card in second 1, then a giant number (6#9).
- The brand mark built from construction guides in 2.5s (2#3).
- A physical prop that states the problem before anyone speaks (2#9, the missing poster).

## Moves that make it

- **Motion by camera, cursor and morph, not cuts.** The top launch films keep about 75% moving with few cuts. Save 0.1 to 0.5s flash cuts for one burst right before the reveal (5#7, 5#16).
- **Make the metaphor the structure.** Every UI beat is a behaviour of one human analogy. 5#1's "AI employee" joins Slack, reads everything, sat in every meeting and never sleeps.
- **Chaos to one window:** N windows cascade, then collapse into the product's single window (5#4).
- **Verb, prompt, result, three times:** a verb card, a real prompt in a real agent, and a concrete result. Run it in two different host apps (5#21).
- **One customer threaded through every feature,** so the tour reads as one story (2#18). On supermemory: one user whose memories reappear in each scene.
- **One bubble pair per capability,** with at least one funny, specific example (5#26).
- **A persistent corner HUD** of brand and meta text framing every shot (5#16).
- **Claim, then proof within about 5s:** every headline card is answered by one UI shot (1#2).
- **One ground colour** can hold 15 different scenes together (2#3).
- **Output becomes the logo:** push into what the product just made and let it resolve into the end mark (5#6). Or defocus the last live shot into the lockup (5#1).

## Ending

- The URL or install command on its own card (2 to 3s), then the lockup (3 to 5s): 6#6, 6#8, 6#9, 6#14.
- An availability line, then the logo ("Lindy · Available now", held about 4s, 5#1).
- The product card with its URL, then a one-line parent-brand sign-off (5#4).
- A callback: the opening skit's character returns before the lockup (6#14).
- **Don't end on the logo alone when there is something to do.** The supermemory MCP launch (2#10) ends on its logo although the post carries a docs link. Only 3 of the 8 supermemory films in batch 2 end on a URL or command.

## Pitfalls

- Opening on a held UI still (2.4s in 2#17), a fade from black (3#24) or a logo sting.
- UI type too small to read on a phone (2#21, 2#23).
- Mastering at -7 LUFS (1#6) or leaving it effectively silent (-34.9 in 2#17).
- Fast flicker cutting for the whole film. Keep it to a 2 to 4s opener (6#13).
- Another company's logo in your title ("X for Y" in 4#3, 4#23, 6#24), which carries impersonation and legal risk.
- A "×" co-brand lockup without a real partnership.

## Build it here

1. Story and claims: `references/story.md`, with the problem first. Write the claims table before the beat map (SKILL.md section 3).
2. Beat map on the grid.
   - UI-led launches use `engine/console-film/`: vector console, typeset terminal, a 160 BPM grid, a synth score, and the brand film as a background cut on the hits.
   - Explainer-style launches use `engine/reference-16x9.swift`.
3. Motion from `motion-grammar.md`: flood and shrink at the turn, state morphs, camera push and pull, text rising from a mask line.
4. QA from `qa.md`:
   - 70% or more moving;
   - longest still about 1s before the end card;
   - -14 LUFS;
   - readable at 390px;
   - an identical final hold.
5. Measure the reference you are borrowing from with `engine/tools/study_video.py` first.
